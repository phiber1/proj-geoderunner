An Abene game for the TRS-80 MC-10

Geode Runner: dig, climb and run.
Grab every geode and escape the mine.

150 levels of ladders, cables, bricks and guards. Every level has an escape solution, and the best ones are puzzles you'll want to solve again and again.

Getting started

Needs an MC-10 with the 8K internal RAM mod and an MCX32-SD, or the VMC10 for Linux emulator run with --8k --mcx32sd. Copy GEODE.BIN and LEVELS.DAT to the SD card and type RUNM"GEODE". Press SPACE on the title screen to start.

Controls

A SWalk left and right, or hang along a cable.
W ZClimb up and down ladders. Z also lets go of a cable or a ladder.
SPACEDig the brick beside your feet, on the side you face. Hold A or S with it to choose the side.
BREAKGive up the level (it costs a life).

A joystick works through a PiKey-10, which maps it to the same keys.

The goal

Collect every geode on the level. When the last one is yours, the exit ladder grows up to the top of the screen. Climb it out to finish the level and earn a bonus and an extra life.

Digging

  • You dig the brick next to your feet, never the one under you. Stone can't be dug, and neither can the brick under a ladder or a geode.
  • A hole stays open for about five seconds, then fills in again. Anything still inside it when it closes is buried.
  • To go down through a floor, dig beside you, and step into the hole to fall to the floor below.
  • Some geodes sit in sealed brick vaults. Working out how to dig your way in, and out again, is part of the puzzle.

The guards

  • Guards chase you everywhere. If one touches you, you lose a life.
  • A guard who walks into a hole is trapped for a few seconds, then climbs out. You can walk across a trapped guard's head, but don't drop into the hole beside his.
  • If a hole fills in with a guard inside, he's buried and reappears somewhere along the top of the screen.
  • Guards pick up geodes they walk over. A trapped guard drops the one he carries. Otherwise he puts it down after a while, wherever he can.
  • A geode carried by a guard to where you can never reach him, such as a pit with no way out, is lost, and so is the level.

Scoring

A geode250
A guard buried in a hole75
Escaping a level1,500 and an extra life

You start with three lives. The title screen shows the best score.

Hints

  • The guards don't move until you do. Study the whole level before you take your first step.
  • Lead guards over a row of holes and you can catch several at once.
  • Find places where you can keep the guards circling while you plan your next move.
  • Watch which guard is carrying a geode before you trap him for good.
  • There's a secret password for choosing the level you start at. Can you find it?
